About

18 years in the bay

I started as an assistant editor at a commercial post house, prepping dailies and conforming spots for broadcast. Since then I've cut national campaigns, launch films and social cutdowns for agencies and in-house brand teams.

Most of my work starts with a script and a pile of footage and ends with a :30, a :15, a :06 and three aspect ratios. I like that problem. Finding the version of a story that works at every length is the craft.

I work out of my own suite in Colorado and remotely with teams anywhere, with fast turnarounds and clean handoffs to color, sound and finishing.
