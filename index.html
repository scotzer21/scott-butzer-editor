About

18 years in the bay

Scott Butzer is an award-winning editor with a career spanning over 18 years in platforms ranging from commercials, music videos, documentaries, and more. Scott is known for his unique ability to work in a wide variety of genres ranging from visual storytelling, to comedy, to automotive, as well as many others.

Scott’s work has been honored throughout the industry, receiving AICP awards for the Vince Staples’ Fun! music video and Frito-Lay’s Taste of Soccer. Scott’s work for Google’s 2020 Year in Search was awarded multiple Webby Awards among others and has amassed over 250 million views worldwide.

Before going freelance, Scott worked out of Los Angeles at Whitehouse Post, Final Cut, and Cabin Editing Company. Butzer has worked with ad agencies and directors, including Wieden + Kennedy; Goodby, Silverstein & Partners; Apple; We Are From LA, Marcus Söderlund, and Jonathan Krisel.
